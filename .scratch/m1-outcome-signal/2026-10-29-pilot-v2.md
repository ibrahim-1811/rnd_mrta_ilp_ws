## 3. Requirement 3 — Exact Metric & Denominator Definitions, Line Citations, and `margin_s < 0` Audit

All formulas below cite exact lines in `src/simulation_env/simulation_env/outcome_signal.py` (SHA256 `6ed886d4...`):

1. **Decision-time static finish estimate (`est_finish_s`, `L67–71`, `L80–82`, `L123–124`)**:
   $$\text{est\_travel\_s}(r, o) = \frac{c_{\text{static\_m}}(r, o)}{v}, \quad \text{est\_service\_s}(o) = k_{\text{shelf}}(o)\cdot \tau_{\text{pick}} + \tau_{\text{drop}}, \quad \text{est\_finish\_s}(r, o) = \text{est\_travel\_s}(r, o) + \text{est\_service\_s}(o)$$
   where `_timing(manifest)` (`L67–71`) reads $v = 1.5\text{ m/s}$, $\tau_{\text{pick}} = 1.0\text{ s}$, $\tau_{\text{drop}} = 1.0\text{ s}$ on active `W100` (`run.json`), and $c_{\text{static\_m}}$ is the exact directed A* mission distance in meters (including legal entry/retreat).

2. **Realized mission duration & residual decomposition (`mission_s`, `residual_s`, `L125–128`, `L230–235`)**:
   $$\text{mission\_s} = t_{\text{retreat\_finished}} - t_{\text{launched}} \quad (\text{L125}), \qquad \text{residual\_s} = \text{mission\_s} - \text{est\_finish\_s} \quad (\text{L126})$$
   $$\text{residual\_s} = \underbrace{\text{realized\_waiting\_s}}_{\text{traffic stops/queues}} + \underbrace{\left(\frac{\text{realized\_distance\_m}}{v} - \text{est\_travel\_s}\right)}_{\text{extra\_travel\_s (L127)}} + \underbrace{\left(\text{realized\_service\_s} - \text{est\_service\_s}\right)}_{\text{service\_diff\_s (L128)}}$$
   - **Empirical check across all `5,400` orders (`18` probes)**: `max_abs_extra_travel_s = 7.93e-12 s`, `max_abs_service_diff_s = 0.0 s`, and `max_abs_decomposition_error_s = 7.84e-12 s`. Because robots execute fixed directed A* paths without dynamic detours and deterministic service durations, **100.000% of `residual_s` is realized traffic waiting** (`residual_from_waiting = 1.0` on all 18 runs).

3. **Run-level `residual_share_of_mission` vs per-order `residual_ratio` (`L129`, `L226`)**:
   $$\text{residual\_share\_of\_mission} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{residual\_s}_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{N} \text{mission\_s}_i} \quad (N = 300, \text{ L226}), \qquad \text{residual\_ratio}_i = \frac{\text{residual\_s}_i}{\text{est\_finish\_s}_i} \quad (\text{L129})$$
   `residual_share_of_mission` is a mission-duration-weighted ratio of totals bounded in $[0, 1)$ when $\text{residual\_s} \ge 0$, whereas `residual_ratio` divides by static `est_finish_s` and can exceed `1.0` on short orders caught in severe jams. For example, on `r100_q3_s101`, `residual_share_of_mission = 23.93%`, mean per-order `residual_ratio = 36.21%`, and unweighted mean $(\text{residual\_s}_i / \text{mission\_s}_i) = 16.75\%$.

4. **Static margin (`margin_s`, `L98–108`) and two-mechanism audit of `margin_s < 0`**:
   For each `(decision_id, robot_id)` group in `decisions.parquet`, let $\mathcal{O}_{\text{other}}(d, r) = \{o' \neq o^*(d,r) : (d, r, o') \text{ eligible in } d\}$ (`others = group[~group["selected"]]["est_finish_s"]`, `L99`):
   $$\text{margin\_s}(d, r) = \begin{cases} \text{NaN} & \text{if } |\mathcal{O}_{\text{other}}(d, r)| = 0 \text{ (i.e. } \text{robot\_options} = 1\text{)}, \\ \min_{o' \in \mathcal{O}_{\text{other}}(d, r)} \text{est\_finish\_s}(r, o') - \text{est\_finish\_s}(r, o^*(d, r)) & \text{otherwise.} \end{cases}$$
   Across all `5,400` orders (`runs/m1_pilot_v2/analysis_csvs/margin_negative_audit.csv`):
   - **`margin_s` is `NaN` on `10 / 5,400` orders (`0.19%`)**: `0` or `1` order per run, occurring only at the final decision when the last pending order is assigned (`robot_options == 1`).
   - **`margin_s < 0` occurs on `2,388 / 5,400` orders (`44.22%`)** due to two distinct mechanisms:
     1. **Multi-robot batch contention (`2,058 / 2,388 = 86.18%`)**: Because `others` (`L99`) includes *all* eligible window orders at decision $d$ — including orders $o'$ claimed by *other* robots earlier in the same greedy batch (`best_other_taken_in_batch == True`) — whenever robot $r_2$ wanted order $o_1$ (`c_static_m(r_2, o_1) < c_static_m(r_2, o_2)`) that robot $r_1$ already took, $\text{margin\_s}(d, r_2) < 0$. This rises strongly with batch size (`q=0 -> q=3` and `30r -> 100r`, e.g., `176–193 / 300` orders at `100r q3`).
     2. **Shelf-pick service-time inversion (`330 / 2,388 = 13.82%` in raw `margin_s`; `389 / 5,400` when restricting `others` to batch-unclaimed orders)**: `nearest_astar` (`src/task_management/task_management/allocation_strategy.py`) scores pairs strictly by `c_static_m` (ignoring `order_shelf_count`), whereas `est_finish_s` adds `1.0 s` per shelf pick on `W100`. If $o^*$ has `3` shelves (`+3.0 s` pick) and unclaimed $o'$ has `1` shelf (`+1.0 s` pick) with $0 \le c_{\text{static\_m}}(r, o') - c_{\text{static\_m}}(r, o^*) < 3.0\text{ m}$ (`< 2.0 s` travel), `nearest_astar` selects $o^*$ while $\text{est\_finish\_s}(r, o') < \text{est\_finish\_s}(r, o^*)$, yielding $\text{margin\_s} \in [-2.0\text{ s}, 0\text{ s})$.

5. **`exceeds_margin_share` and exact denominator $n_{\text{alt}}$ (`L130`, `L211`, `L237–239`)**:
   $$\mathcal{A} = \{i \in \{1,\dots,300\} : \text{margin\_s}_i \neq \text{NaN} \;\land\; \text{margin\_s}_i \ge 0\}, \quad n_{\text{alt}} = |\mathcal{A}|, \quad \text{exceeds\_margin\_share} = \frac{1}{n_{\text{alt}}} \sum_{i \in \mathcal{A}} \mathbb{I}[\text{residual\_s}_i > \text{margin\_s}_i]$$
   Filtering to $\text{margin\_s} \ge 0$ (`L211`) prevents trivially counting $\text{residual\_s} = 0 > \text{margin\_s}$ when $\text{margin\_s} < 0$. As a sensitivity check (`margin_negative_audit.csv`), if $\mathcal{O}_{\text{other}}(d, r)$ is restricted to *batch-unclaimed* orders, $n_{\text{alt}}$ increases from `3,002 / 5,400` to `3,779 / 5,400` (`1,232 NaN` when the `t=0` launch batch claims all remaining window orders + `389 < 0` pure shelf inversions), while `exceeds_margin_share` remains nearly identical (`6.9% -> 7.0%` at `30r q0`, `10.0% -> 10.2%` at `30r q3`, `31.5% -> 31.7%` at `70r q0`, `37.8% -> 37.3%` at `70r q3`, `45.7% -> 46.8%` at `100r q0`, `55.2% -> 56.1%` at `100r q3`).

### 5.2 Requirement 6 — Reconciling Observational Variability vs Causal Decision Improvement

Comparing Sections 2–3 against Table 5.1 directly answers Requirement 6:
1. **Why observational `exceeds_margin_share` (`34.45%` on `r70_q3_s102`, `50.00%` on `r100_q3_s102`) is much larger than pure single-pivot mission flips at moderate density (`12.5%` on `r70_q3_s102`)**:
   - **Common-mode congestion**: Observational `exceeds_margin` tests `residual_s(teacher) > est_finish_s(alt) - est_finish_s(teacher)` *implicitly assuming the alternative experiences zero congestion*. In reality, when warehouse traffic is broadly distributed (`r100_q3_s102` `d=34`, where `teacher` and `alt1` both have `est_s = 91.33 s` and both suffer `2.00 s` waiting, yielding `93.33 s` each; or `d=46`, where `alt1` suffers `13.00 s` waiting vs teacher's `0.33 s`), congestion delays alternatives too!
   - **Batch-contention distortion in raw `margin_s`**: In Table 5.1A (`r70_q3_s102`), `6/8` probed decisions had `obs_teacher_margin_s < 0` (`d=5, 14, 23, 32, 41, 68`) only because another robot in the same batch took a closer order; among *unclaimed* alternatives (`static_margin_to_alt1_s`), the true static gap was `+1.0 s` to `+8.33 s`, larger than teacher's mild `0.0–2.0 s` residual on `s102`.
2. **Where causal gains ARE large and actionable**:
   - **Localized corridor jams at high density (`r100_q3_s102` `d=4, 10`)**: When teacher's chosen route enters an acute space-time bottleneck (`obs_teacher_residual_s = 52.33 s` at `d=4`, `40.33 s` at `d=10`) while an alternative with a small static gap (`+1.00 s` at `d=4`, `+1.67 s` at `d=10`) routes into a clear corridor, forcing the alternative yields **simultaneous Pareto breakthroughs**: `-50.33 s` pivot mission AND `-165.33 s` fleet waiting (`d=4`), and `-14.00 s` pivot mission AND `-119.00 s` fleet waiting (`d=10`).
   - **Shelf-pick static blindness (`r100_q3_s102` `d=22`)**: Because `nearest_astar` ignores `1.0 s/shelf` pick time, `alt1` (`O0197`) is already `1.00 s` faster statically (`84.67 s` vs `85.67 s`) and saves another `2.33 s` pivot wait (`-3.33 s` total) plus `-21.33 s` fleet waiting.
   - **Fleet-waiting externalities (`d=5, 32, 41` in `70r`; `d=28` in `100r`)**: Even when the pivot robot itself takes `+7.7 s` to `+18.0 s` longer on an uncongested longer route, keeping that robot out of a congested trunk aisle saves `16.3 s` to `47.3 s` of waiting across other robots in the next `120 s` (and completes `+2` extra orders inside $H$ at `70r d=5` and `100r d=28`).
3. **Why `window_flow_s` (`outcome_signal.py:265–331`) fails to capture causal single-decision value**:
   - `window_flow_s` averages `completed_at - t_d` across all `W` open window orders (`W = 90–100` in early/mid `100r`). Swapping `pivot_robot` from `O0172` to `O0180` at `100r d=4` saves `50.33 s` on `pivot_robot` and `165.33 s` of fleet waiting, **yet `window_flow_s` worsens by `+1.30 s` (`166.54 -> 167.84 s`)** because `O0172` sits unassigned in the queue until a later batch! Conversely, tiny queue-reordering noise shifts `window_flow_s` by `0.04–0.78 s` (`62.5%` raw flips in `rollout_signal/summary.csv`, `0%` flips `> 5 s`). Thus `J = pivot_mission_s + lambda * waiting_in_h_s` is the right causal target; `window_flow_s` is too diluted and queue-confounded for per-decision supervision.

## 8. Preliminary Recommendations for M1 Acceptance Gate & M2/M6 Design (`H, \lambda, \delta, J`)

*(Subject to human researcher review; derived strictly from training seeds `101–103`.)*

1. **M1 Scientific Gate Verdict — GO (Targeted to Medium/High Density `>= 70` Robots)**:
   - At `30` robots (`residual_share_of_mission = 0.90%` at `q=0`, `1.87%` at `q=3`), static routing (`est_finish_s`) is nearly exact; an outcome-aware rule should **gracefully fall back to static `est_finish_s`** (which itself already improves on `nearest_astar`'s `c_static_m` by accounting for `1.0 s/shelf` service time!).
   - At `70–100` robots (`residual_share_of_mission = 7.75%` at `70r q3`, `17.61%` at `100r q3`; `exceeds_margin_share = 37.8%` to `55.2%`), both observational residuals and causal single-pivot rollouts demonstrate large, repeatable improvements over `nearest_astar`.

2. **Recommended Counterfactual Labeling Objective $(H, \lambda, \delta, J)$ for M6 (Outcome-Aware ILP)**:
   - **Horizon $H = 120\text{ s}$**: Covers `1.2x–1.5x` of mean realized mission duration (`81–102 s`) so the pivot mission almost always completes inside $H$ while capturing downstream corridor queueing over a fixed, branch-invariant time window `[t_d, first tick >= t_d + 120 s]`.
   - **Objective $J(b) = \text{pivot\_mission\_s}(b) + \lambda \cdot \text{waiting\_in\_h\_s}(b)$ (reject `window_flow_s`)**: `window_flow_s` dilutes single-pivot causal gains by $1/W \approx 1/95$ and suffers from unassigned-order queue-wait confounding (Section 5.2).
   - **Recommended weight $\lambda \in [0.25, 0.50]$ (primary $\lambda = 0.50$, conservative sensitivity $\lambda = 0.25$)**:
     - $\lambda = 0.0$ ignores real `16–47 s` fleet-waiting externalities (`d=5, 32, 41` at `70r`; `d=28` at `100r`).
     - $\lambda = 1.0$ allows a branch to sacrifice `+18 s` of pivot mission for `19 s` of fleet waiting across `99` other robots (`0.19 s/robot`), over-rotating away from static efficiency.
     - **$\lambda = 0.50$** requires `2.0 s` of fleet-wide waiting saved to justify `1.0 s` of extra pivot mission time, cleanly capturing all `3` Pareto jam-avoidance wins AND the `47.3 s` externality win at `100r` (`50.0%` flip rate at `100r`, `37.5%` at `70r` for $\delta = 2\text{ s}$).
   - **Recommended minimum improvement margin $\delta = 2.0\text{ s}$ (`6` simulation ticks, with $\delta = 5.0\text{ s}$ high-confidence tier / example weight)**:
     - Filters out `1–3` tick discretization chatter (`0.33–1.00 s`) while retaining `7/16` (`43.8%`) pooled `70r+100r` improvements at $\lambda = 0.50$ (`5/16 = 31.3%` at $\delta = 5.0\text{ s}$). Example weights $w_d = \max(0, \Delta J_d - \delta)$ naturally up-weight `50–133 s` jam-avoidance wins over `2–3 s` borderline flips.

3. **Recommended M2–M6 Decision-Time Predicate Vocabulary**:
   - **Include (Static + Load)**: `est_finish_s` (or `c_static_m` + `order_shelf_count`), batch-unclaimed `margin_est_s`, `free_robots`, `window_orders`.
   - **Include (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Surviving Congestion Predicates — prefer *within-decision relative deltas* $\Delta f = f(r, o) - \min_{o'} f(r, o')$)**: `st_conflict_robots`, `occupied_now`, `st_conflicts_30s` (or `st_conflicts`), `local_density`, and `overlap_ratio`. Using within-decision relative predicates automatically eliminates the `t = 0` cold-start offset (`f = 0` for all candidates at `t = 0`).
   - **Exclude / Guard**: Exclude raw `route_cells` and raw `overlap_cells` from congestion predicates (collinear/inverted with route length), exclude `shelf_load` (sparse/null), and **forbid `robot_soc_percent` from congestion rules** (spurious run-clock proxy on non-charging horizons; restrict battery predicates strictly to energy-reserve margins on charging horizons `>= 1,000` orders if Battery V1 enters M2–M6).
